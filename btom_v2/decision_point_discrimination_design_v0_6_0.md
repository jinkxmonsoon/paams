# Prospectively frozen scenario-discrimination experiment v0.6.0

This stage validates state-contingent behavior under record-framed references before treatment effects are tested. Parent run 30372084567 showed saturated actions and three rate limits; H1a, H1b, and H2 remain untested.

Four H1 route-map variants balance whether the first or second listed route reaches the current room. Representation consistency—not objective correctness—is the outcome in stale cases. Four H2 variants trade a corrective message against immediate progress, with an explicit one-decision communication cost.

Acceptance uses only `matched_decision_record` for H1 and `self_belief_plus_message_record` for H2. A family passes when at least three of four reference variants show the prospectively defined paired discrimination. Treatment outputs are descriptive and cannot affect retention. The 3/4 rule is a validation heuristic, not statistical significance. No effect size or inferential hypothesis test is performed.
