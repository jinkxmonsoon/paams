## Frozen bank

The bank contains 18 new variants, one per six-archetype by three-difficulty cell, and four record-only `M_R × M_I` prompts per variant (72 prompts). The label is exactly `Current partner record:`. Each scenario has a focal agent, an addressed partner with an immediate assignment, and a named operator executing an independent assignment. Messages reach the addressed partner only, are not relayed, cannot alter the operator's assignment, and the episode ends after both assignments execute. The branches share no object, target, route, or action.

Direct and distractor variants use matched one-edge propositions. Distractor variants add one fixed contextual fact. Compositional variants use matched two-edge entity-to-intermediate-to-value propositions. Only final record values vary across cells. All false alternatives are plausible, same-type, and distinct. Opportunity-cost wording and the two strict actions are inherited from the validated development macro protocol.

The holdout entity/value namespace is disjoint from all 18 earlier development realizations and all 360 candidate realizations. Candidate selection is never read. Semantic signatures hash normalized model-visible invariant content, excluding IDs, metadata, and cell-specific record values.

## Future gates and stop rules

The original five scenario-control gates remain unchanged: pooled relevance ≥ 0.25; both `M_I`-specific relevance effects > 0; and both absolute irrelevant-mismatch effects ≤ 0.25. Separately, a heuristic measurement-headroom warning is raised if either pooled `M_R` rate is ≤ 0.05 or ≥ 0.95. It is not a sixth test and cannot alter variants.

Future classifications are frozen: `record_holdout_validated`, `generic_mismatch_salience_replication`, `record_holdout_relevance_failure`, or `causal_control_valid_but_measurement_saturated`. Confirmatory realization is permitted only after all five gates pass without the headroom warning.
